Forward Notice

Tokenisation and Digital Payment Regulation

A monthly register of the rules that create deadlines or obligations for blockchain infrastructure vendors: oracle networks, cross-chain interoperability, proof-of-reserve and NAV reporting, and tokenised fund workflow. Each entry says whether the rule binds the vendor directly, reaches it through regulated customers, creates demand, or constrains design.

      How to read this

      Horizon is measured from the update date to the next dated milestone: 0-6m, 6-18m, 18m+, or no future date where an instrument already applies and no further milestone is confirmed.

      Every date is taken from the regulator, legislature, official journal or standard setter. Where a source gives a relative deadline (for example, 60 days after Federal Register publication), the register records it as stated rather than calculating a date. Confidence reflects how firmly the primary source supports the entry.

      This register describes regulation in general terms for infrastructure vendors as a class. It is not legal advice, and whether a rule applies to any specific firm depends on what that firm actually does and where.
